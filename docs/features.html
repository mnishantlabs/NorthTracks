everything a music library deserves

northtracks is built around one idea — your collection lives on your disk, and every tool here exists to make it behave like you own it.

library
✓local scanning — reads mp3, m4a, flac and wav from folders you choose.
✓embedded metadata — titles, artists, albums and genres pulled from the files themselves.
✓duplicate detection — matches title + artist and resolves dupes automatically.
✓genre organization — copies music into genre-sorted folders at your destination of choice.
✓global search — instant lookup across title, artist and genre.
✓liked songs — a persistent collection you curate with one click.
player
✓full queue system — build, reorder and save your own queue.
✓shuffle & smart shuffle — plus repeat modes and volume control.
✓now playing view — full-screen immersive view with an up-next queue.
✓smart home screen — recently played, top songs and daily recommendations.
✓playlists — auto-generated genre playlists plus your own custom ones.
✓keyboard & wheel — seek with the scroll wheel, control everything from your keyboard.
ui & feel
✓light & dark mode — with system preference detection.
✓accent color — pick the color that feels like you.
✓font selection — choose how everything reads.
✓windows 11 styles — solid, acrylic and acrylic with cover glow.
✓immersive banners — artist and genre pages with full-bleed hero imagery.
✓context menus — right-click playback, playlist and file actions everywhere.
integration
✓discord rich presence — show what you're playing.
✓media controls — windows system media transport controls built in.
✓taskbar thumbnails — playback buttons right on the taskbar preview.
✓fully offline — zero internet, zero telemetry, zero account.
tech stack
electron react typescript vite electron-store music-metadata chokidar
requirements

windows 10 or windows 11 — 64-bit

everything else lives inside the installer. download it from the catalog and you're set.
