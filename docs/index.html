Everything your library needs

Scan & Metadata

Reads embedded metadata from MP3, M4A, FLAC, and WAV files in your local folders.

Duplicate Detection

Automatically finds and resolves duplicate tracks by matching title and artist.

Genre Sorting

Copies and organizes your music into genre-sorted folders at a destination of your choice.

Full Music Player

Queue, shuffle, smart shuffle, repeat, and volume control with a full-screen Now Playing view.

Smart Home Screen

Recently Played, Top Songs, and daily recommendations built from your listening history.

Playlists & Liked

Auto-generated genre playlists, custom playlists, and a Liked Songs collection.

Global Search

Instant search across title, artist, and genre anywhere in your library.

Rich Presence

Discord Rich Presence, Windows media controls, and taskbar thumbnail buttons.

Customizable UI

Light and dark mode, accent colors, fonts, and Windows 11 Acrylic visual styles.

Built with modern tooling

Electron React TypeScript Vite electron-store music-metadata chokidar

NorthTracks is fully offline. No internet required after installation.
